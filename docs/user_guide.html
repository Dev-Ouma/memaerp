1. Executive Summary & Core Architecture

The MEMA ERP Academic Management Suite executes the applicant-to-student journey through a zero-data-loss, cryptographically verifiable funnel. Unlike legacy systems that duplicate records or drop context upon enrollment, MEMA ERP maintains single-identity continuity: the user who drafts the application retains their audit history, login credentials, and stakeholder permissions when elevated to an official student.

Key Architectural Pillars
1. Single Identity Continuity: An applicant account is directly promoted into a student account without creating duplicate login profiles.
2. Cryptographic Tamper-Evidence: Every submission snapshot and offer acceptance is sealed with a SHA-256 evidence hash.
3. Atomic Idempotent Conversion: Database row locks prevent dual student number allocations during concurrent registration surges.

3. Step-by-Step Lifecycle Guide

STAGE 1 Public Intake & Application Submission

The prospective student accesses /admissions/my-application, creates an account, and selects an active intake (e.g. September 2026 Intake).

  • Biodata & Academic History: Candidate completes primary, secondary (KCSE), and tertiary educational records.
  • Document Uploads: National ID, KCSE result slips, passport photos, and leaving certificates are uploaded to ApplicationDocument.
  • Application Fee Payment: KES 1,000 application fee is validated via MPESA / bank integration (ApplicationPaymentAttempt).
  • Cryptographic Sealing: Upon submission, the system generates a SHA-256 hashed snapshot (ApplicationVersion), issues receipt MC-REC-YYYY-XXXXX, and transitions status: DRAFT → SUBMITTED.
STAGE 2 Admissions Office Triage & Document Verification

Admissions clerks operate from the live work queues at /admissions/document-verification.

  • Desk Locking: Clicking Claim File assigns the dossier to the officer, preventing dual-processing.
  • Verification Checks: Officer cross-checks certificates against national databases and minimum course requirements.
  • Correction Requests: If a document is defective, the officer triggers RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION or INFO_REQUESTED with specific feedback.
  • Advancement: Satisfactory applications are marked verified: SUBMITTED → VERIFIED.
STAGE 3 Departmental Scoring, Review & Shortlisting

Academic departments evaluate candidates at /admissions/reviews and /admissions/shortlists.

  • Scoring Rubric: Evaluation based on Mean Grade (0–50 pts), Cluster Subjects (0–30 pts), and Affirmative Action/Talents (0–20 pts).
  • Shortlisting: Applicants meeting cutoff points advance to SHORTLISTED.
  • Waitlist Management: Qualified candidates exceeding current intake quotas are placed on WAITLISTED with automatic queue promotion.
STAGE 4 Institutional Approvals & Offer Letter Issuance

The Faculty Dean and Registrar review approval batches at /admissions/approvals.

  • Formal Sign-Off: Batch approval transitions candidates to ADMITTED.
  • Automated Offer Generation: System allocates unique Offer Number (e.g., MC/ADM/202609/A8F19B20) and generates a 48-character QR verification token.
  • Admission Letter PDF: Generates official university admission package containing reporting dates, campus guidelines, and fee requirements.
STAGE 5 Offer Response, Acceptance & Legal Commitment

The applicant logs into /applicant/applications/{id}/offer to review and accept their offer.

  • Forensic Non-Repudiation: Acceptance captures legal proof: client IP address, browser User-Agent, UTC timestamp, and SHA-256 evidence hash.
  • Status Progression: Automatic advancement: ADMITTED → ACCEPTED → READY_TO_ENROL.
STAGE 6 Finance Clearance & Fee Reconciliation

The Finance Office validates tuition payments at /fees/pending-payments and /admissions/payments.

  • Semester Invoicing: Invoices for Year 1 Semester 1 tuition, caution fees, library levy, and student ID are generated.
  • Payment Verification: Bank slips / Paybill transactions are matched and confirmed, granting financial clearance.
STAGE 7 Student Conversion & Permanent Identity Materialization

Executed via /admissions/student-conversions or action /admissions/applications/{id}/convert by the Registrar.

Mechanics of StudentConversionService
StudentConversionService performs an atomic database transaction:
1. Obtains a pessimistic lock on the application and course records.
2. Increments courses.next_student_serial non-atomically within the lock.
3. Formats permanent Admission Number: BCS/042/2026 (Course / Serial / Year).
4. Inserts permanent row in students linked to academic_sessions.
5. Upgrades User.role = 'student' and adds stakeholder permission.
6. Updates application status to ENROLLED.
STAGE 8 Cohort Mapping, Stream Allocation & Unit Enrollment

Academic Affairs links the student to classes and curriculum modules.

  • Cohort Assignment: Student is linked to Cohort 2026/2027, Stage Year 1 Semester 1, and Stream A (/cohort/programme-cohort-mapping).
  • Course Unit Registration: Student is registered for standard curriculum units (e.g. Introduction to Programming, Discrete Mathematics, Computer Architecture) via /registration/student-registrations.
STAGE 9 Classroom Readiness, LMS Sync & Academic Attendance

The student enters the active learning environment.

  • LMS / Moodle Provisioning: Automated sync provisions course shells, syllabi, and assignment portals (/lms/course-shells).
  • Timetable & Attendance: Classes appear on the student timetable. Lecturers capture live attendance (AttendanceRecord).
  • Examinations & Progression: Student sits continuous assessments, semester exams (/examination/exam-schedule), and receives Senate-approved transcripts.

4. Exception Handling & Troubleshooting Directory

Condition / Issue Root Cause Resolution Procedure
Application submission disabled Application fee unpaid or required documents missing. Verify KES 1,000 transaction on /admissions/payments. Ensure all required upload fields are satisfied.
Expired admission offer Candidate failed to accept before deadline. Admissions Officer opens /admissions/offers, edits expires_at timestamp, and re-notifies applicant.
Conversion FAILED in table Programme offering missing linked course_id. Navigate to /admissions/student-conversions. Fix course association, then click Retry Conversion.
Course units missing in portal Programme cohort mapping not published. Navigate to /cohort/programme-cohort-mapping and assign active curriculum units to the cohort.
LMS courses not accessible Moodle synchronization job queued. Open /registration/moodle-sync and trigger manual sync to immediately push registered student shells.
Enterprise Governance & Audit Assurance
All state transitions, IP addresses, execution channels, and staff decisions are permanently recorded in the live PostgreSQL audit ledger. Access the real-time compliance dashboard at /reports/audit-trail-user.
